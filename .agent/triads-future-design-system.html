Design System — v2 Proposal

Quiet confidence, executed properly this time.

Same brand thesis as before — monochrome, editorial, business-partner tone — rebuilt with a distinct palette, a three-role type system, and a signature device drawn from the brand's own name.

Colour

01 / Strictly monochrome

Decision 014 is explicit: black, white, and neutral tones only — colour comes from photography and client work, not the interface. So this palette adds no brand accent at all. That restraint is itself the choice: cooler and greyer than the old warm-cream build, so it reads as considered rather than a template default.

Typography

02 / Three roles, one risk

The old build paired two geometric sans faces (Inter + Outfit) — safe, but flat. Here, headlines get a structured grotesk built for confidence; long-form reading gets a literary serif built for storytelling; UI chrome stays a tight, quiet sans. The serif-for-body / sans-for-headline split is the deliberate reversal of the usual pattern.

The Triad Mark

03 / Signature element
StrategyWhere growth is planned
TechnologyWhere growth is built
ExecutionWhere growth compounds

Three ascending bars, standing in for the three things Triads Future actually says it does together: strategy, technology, execution. It's not a decorative "01/02/03" — it's the brand's real approach, made visible.

The same mark doubles as the language for your three real device experiences — Desktop, Tablet, Mobile aren't a compressed/expanded pair, they're three deliberate designs, same as the three bars. It shows up as a section-transition rule on long pages, and quietly, as a wordmark companion next to "Triads Future."

Buttons

04 / One primary action per page

Per the UX Principles, every page has one dominant CTA. Three styles, no more — pill radius throughout, matching the original DLS instruction that nav/CTA buttons stay pill-shaped while cards and inputs stay sharp.

Cards

05 / Outcomes, not services

Solution cards are written as outcomes (per Decision 003), not service names — pulled directly from the Homepage Blueprint's real content.
